## Legal Standard

11. A party propounding interrogatories may move to compel a
further response when an answer is evasive or incomplete, or when an
objection is without merit or too general. The moving party must
show good cause and a reasonable attempt to resolve the dispute
informally before filing.

12. Boilerplate objections asserted without factual support are
improper, and their repetition across an entire set of responses
supports a finding that the responding party did not act with the
diligence the statute requires of a responding party.
