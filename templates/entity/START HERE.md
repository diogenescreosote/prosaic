This folder is the official record of **Example Foundation**: how it was
formed, the rules it runs by, what its board decided, who holds office,
its tax standing, and its dealings with government agencies. It is
kept so that anyone can find any of that without help.

## How to read this folder

The records are in **`assets/`**, in numbered sections:

| Section | What is in it |
|---|---|
| `01 Formation` | The filing that created the entity, its endorsement by the state, and the first acts that organized it |
| `02 Governing Documents` | Articles (as amended), bylaws (current and superseded), and standing policies the board has adopted |
| `03 Board Minutes and Consents` | Minutes of every board meeting and every written action without a meeting, by date |
| `04 Directors and Officers` | Elections, appointments, resignations, and each person's signed statements (conflict of interest, consent to serve) |
| `05 Other Official Acts` | Acts of committees and other bodies with authority under the governing documents, and officers' certificates |
| `06 Tax Status` | Applications for and determinations of tax exemption, and the employer identification number |
| `07 Government Filings` | Recurring reports to the state and federal agencies: statements of information, registrations, annual returns |
| `08 Litigation` | Any lawsuit or proceeding the entity is party to (empty means none) |
| `09 Finances` | Board-level financial records: adopted budgets, annual financial statements, audits, banking resolutions. The day-to-day books are kept elsewhere |
| `10 Correspondence` | Letters and notices to and from agencies and others |

**`assets/INDEX.md`** lists every document, with its date and its
status: `filed` (bears the agency's endorsement), `acknowledged` (an
agency receipt or confirmation, not the endorsed document),
`as-submitted` (our copy of what was sent, with no agency mark),
`executed` (a signed original or a scan of one), `copy` (an unsigned
copy of something signed elsewhere), or `not-in-hand`. A status is
recorded by a person from the face of the document, never guessed.

**`JOURNAL.md`**: every official act, in order. Each entry says what
was done, by whom, on what vote and authority, and which record in
`assets/` shows it. Entries are added, never erased; a correction is
a new entry or a note saying what changed and why.

Everything else here is working material: `src/` holds drafts (each
stamped DRAFT until adopted), `knowledge/` holds notes, `inbox/` is
where new material arrives before it is filed. None of it is part of
the record.
