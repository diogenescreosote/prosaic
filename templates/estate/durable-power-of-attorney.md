**NOTICE:** THE POWERS GRANTED BY THIS DOCUMENT ARE BROAD AND
SWEEPING. THEY ARE EXPLAINED IN THE UNIFORM STATUTORY FORM POWER OF
ATTORNEY ACT (CALIFORNIA PROBATE CODE SECTIONS 4400-4465). IF YOU
HAVE ANY QUESTIONS ABOUT THESE POWERS, OBTAIN COMPETENT LEGAL
ADVICE. THIS DOCUMENT DOES NOT AUTHORIZE ANYONE TO MAKE MEDICAL AND
OTHER HEALTH-CARE DECISIONS FOR YOU. YOU MAY REVOKE THIS POWER OF
ATTORNEY IF YOU LATER WISH TO DO SO. *[Copy the full statutory
notice from § 4401 verbatim.]*

I, Jane Roe, of Springfield, California, appoint Morgan Reyes as my
agent (attorney-in-fact) to act for me in any lawful way with
respect to the initialed subjects below. If Morgan Reyes is unable
or unwilling to serve, I appoint Sam Turner as successor agent.

**To grant all of the following powers, initial the line in front of
(N) and ignore the lines in front of the other powers. To grant one
or more, but fewer than all, initial the line in front of each power
you are granting.**

____ (A) Real property transactions.
____ (B) Tangible personal property transactions.
____ (C) Stock and bond transactions.
____ (D) Commodity and option transactions.
____ (E) Banking and other financial institution transactions.
____ (F) Business operating transactions.
____ (G) Insurance and annuity transactions.
____ (H) Estate, trust, and other beneficiary transactions.
____ (I) Claims and litigation.
____ (J) Personal and family maintenance.
____ (K) Benefits from social security, medicare, medicaid, or other
governmental programs, or civil or military service.
____ (L) Retirement plan transactions.
____ (M) Tax matters.
____ (N) ALL OF THE POWERS LISTED ABOVE.

**Special instructions:** My agent is specifically authorized to
transfer my assets to the trustee of the Jane Roe Living Trust dated
August 1, 2026, and to complete beneficiary designations naming that
trust, consistent with my estate plan. ______________________.

**Durability.** This power of attorney will continue to be effective
even though I become incapacitated.

I agree that any third party who receives a copy of this document
may act under it. Revocation of the power of attorney is not
effective as to a third party until the third party has actual
knowledge of the revocation.

I sign this instrument on ____________ at Springfield, California.

\signblock{dated}{JANE ROE}{Principal}

\acknowledgment{JANE ROE}
