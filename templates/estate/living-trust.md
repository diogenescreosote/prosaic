# Article I. Identification

I, Jane Roe, residing in California, declare this instrument the
Jane Roe Living Trust dated August 1, 2026 (the "Trust"). I am the
Settlor and the initial Trustee. The Trust is revocable during my
lifetime and becomes irrevocable at my death.

# Article II. Trustee

1. **Initial Trustee.** I serve as Trustee while living and able.
2. **Incapacity.** I am deemed unable to serve upon the earliest of
   (a) my written resignation; (b) written certification by two
   licensed physicians, each of whom has examined me, that I can no
   longer manage my financial affairs; or (c) an order of a court of
   competent jurisdiction. A successor Trustee may rely on such a
   certification without further inquiry.
3. **Successor Trustees**, one at a time, in this order: Morgan
   Reyes; Sam Turner; Riley Chen. A successor accepts office by
   signing a written acceptance.
4. **No Bond.** No Trustee shall be required to furnish bond.
5. **Powers.** Each Trustee has all powers granted trustees under
   the California Probate Code, including to buy, sell, hold,
   manage, lease, and encumber real and personal property; maintain
   bank and brokerage accounts; own and transfer vehicles; collect
   insurance and retirement proceeds; and employ professionals.
6. **Authority of Successors.** A duly acting successor Trustee has
   full authority to hold, manage, transfer, and title Trust assets
   without amendment of this instrument.
7. **Titling.** Title to Trust assets shall be taken in the name of
   *[Trustee's name], Trustee of the Jane Roe Living Trust dated
   August 1, 2026*.

# Article III. Beneficiaries

1. At my death, the Trust Estate shall be distributed in equal
   shares to Morgan Reyes, Alex Roe, and Casey Roe.
2. **Per stirpes.** A deceased beneficiary's share passes to that
   beneficiary's descendants by right of representation; if none, it
   is divided among the surviving named beneficiaries (or their
   descendants, per stirpes).
3. **Young beneficiaries.** Any share for a beneficiary under
   twenty-five is held in further trust, with the Trustee authorized
   to apply income and principal for the beneficiary's health,
   education, maintenance, and support; the remainder is distributed
   outright at twenty-five.

# Article IV. Indivisible Property

1. The Trustee may sell, retain, or distribute indivisible property
   (real estate, vehicles, heirlooms) as reasonably serves the
   beneficiaries' interests.
2. Indivisible property distributed to a beneficiary is credited
   against that beneficiary's share at fair market value.
3. If beneficiaries disagree on a value, the Trustee may engage an
   independent licensed appraiser, whose appraisal binds all
   parties.

# Article V. Pour-Over and After-Acquired Assets

1. Assets transferred to the Trust by my will become part of the
   Trust Estate.
2. My will, and the operative version of every document connected
   with the Trust, may be identified through the Document Manifest
   protocol of Article VIII. The identification is presumptive, not
   dispositive: a will speaks through its own execution under the
   Probate Code.
3. The Trust may receive retirement accounts and insurance proceeds
   designated to it.

# Article VI. Succession and Restrictions

1. While I serve as Trustee I may revoke, amend, or terminate the
   Trust by a writing signed by me.
2. After my death or incapacity, no successor Trustee may revoke or
   amend the Trust.
3. Successor Trustees serve individually, not jointly.

# Article VII. Governing Law and Venue

California law governs this Trust. Any proceeding concerning it
shall be brought in the Superior Court for the County of Example.

2. **Presumption.** Any instrument concerning the Trust — including

   direction to a Trustee, but excluding any will or codicil —
   bearing a cryptographic signature verifiable against the Anchored
   Key and carrying a trusted timestamp shall be presumed authentic

   clear and convincing evidence of forgery, compromise of the key,
   or my lack of capacity or free will at signing.
3. **Timestamps.** A "trusted timestamp" is proof, generated by an
   independent timestamping service or public blockchain anchor,
   that the signature existed at a stated time. A signature without
   one still carries whatever evidentiary weight the law gives it,
   but not the presumption of this Article.
4. **The Manifest.** I may from time to time sign, under the
   Anchored Key, a Document Manifest identifying by cryptographic
   hash the operative versions of documents connected with the
   Trust. The most recent manifest so signed and timestamped
   supersedes all earlier ones and is presumptive evidence of which
   versions are operative.
5. **Revocation.** If I publish a revocation of the Anchored Key, or
   a signed and timestamped instrument declaring it compromised, no
   signature made after the revocation's timestamp receives the
   presumption of this Article. A successor key receives it only if
   anchored by an instrument executed with the formalities of this
   Trust.
6. **Wills excluded.** Nothing in this Article gives testamentary
   effect to any electronic record.

# Article IX. Miscellaneous

1. **No contest.** A beneficiary who contests this Trust forfeits
   their interest, unless the contest was brought with probable
   cause as defined by California law.
2. **Spendthrift.** No beneficiary's interest may be assigned or
   reached by creditors before actual distribution.
3. **Severability.** Invalidity of one provision does not affect the
   remainder.
