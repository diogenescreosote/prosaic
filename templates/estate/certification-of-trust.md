I, Jane Roe, certify under penalty of perjury under the laws of the
State of California:

1. **Existence and date.** The Jane Roe Living Trust was executed on
   August 1, 2026, and is currently in full force and effect.
2. **Settlor.** The settlor of the trust is Jane Roe.
3. **Trustee.** The currently acting trustee is Jane Roe. Successor
   trustees, serving one at a time, are: Morgan Reyes; Sam Turner;
   Riley Chen.
4. **Powers.** The trustee has all powers granted trustees under the
   California Probate Code, including the powers to acquire, sell,
   encumber, lease, and manage real and personal property; to open,
   maintain, and close deposit and brokerage accounts; and to
   collect insurance and retirement proceeds payable to the trust.
5. **Revocability.** The trust is revocable, and the person holding
   the power to revoke it is Jane Roe. Upon the settlor's death the
   trust becomes irrevocable.
6. **Cotrustees.** There are no cotrustees. Any duly acting
   successor trustee acts alone.
7. **Title.** Title to trust assets shall be taken as: *Jane Roe,
   Trustee of the Jane Roe Living Trust, dated August 1, 2026* (or
   the corresponding successor trustee).
8. **Tax identification.** During the settlor's lifetime the trust
   uses the settlor's Social Security number. [Do not print the
   number here.]
9. **No contrary modification.** The trust has not been revoked,
   modified, or amended in any manner that would cause the
   representations in this certification to be incorrect.
10. **Complete copy not required.** This certification is made under
    Probate Code section 18100.5. A copy of relevant excerpts of the
    trust instrument will be furnished on reasonable request; the
    dispositive provisions need not be disclosed.
