This folder holds the current, executed versions of Jane Roe's
estate documents, kept for the people named in them.

## What exists

1. **A Living Trust** (the Jane Roe Living Trust, dated August 1,
   2026) — revocable during Jane's life; it becomes irrevocable at
   her death. It says who manages things (the trustee), who inherits
   (the beneficiaries), and how.
2. **A Will** — a "pour-over" will: anything Jane owned that wasn't
   already in the Trust is directed into it. It also nominates a
   guardian for any child under eighteen.
3. **A Certification of Trust** — the short document banks and title
   companies actually want. Hand them this, not the Trust.

The signed originals are in `Executed Documents/`. This folder is a
reading copy for you — the master records are kept separately, so
never worry about breaking anything here.

## Who does what

  first willing person on that list takes over the Trust — read the
  Trust's Article II for how that works, and take the Certification

- **Guardian nominee** for any child under eighteen: Morgan Reyes.

## If Jane has died

1. Order several certified copies of the death certificate; put a
   scan of one in `Inbox/` here.
2. The successor trustee reads the Trust, then contacts the
   institutions holding trust assets with the Certification of
   Trust and the death certificate.
3. The executor consults a probate attorney about whether the Will
   needs to be filed (California requires lodging the will with the
   court; small estates may avoid full probate).
4. Anything you're unsure about, put a note or document in
   `Inbox/`.

## About this folder

- `Executed Documents/` — the signed instruments. Clean copies.
- `Verification/` — for the technically inclined: how to prove these
  documents are authentic and unaltered, plus the cryptographic
  materials that make that possible. Most readers never need it.
- `Inbox/` — writable by the people this folder is shared with: put
  death certificates, funding documents, or questions here.
- `Public/` — documents intended for the world: recorded deeds and
  anything slated for recording.

This folder is maintained with [prosaic](https://github.com/diogenescreosote/prosaic),
a plain-files legal document system; the folder you are reading is
its published output, not its workshop.
