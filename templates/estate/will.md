# Article I. Declaration

I, Jane Roe, residing in Springfield, California, being of sound
mind, declare this to be my Last Will and Testament. I revoke all
prior wills and codicils.

# Article II. The Trust

I am the settlor and initial trustee of the Jane Roe Living Trust
dated August 1, 2026 (as amended from time to time, the "Trust").
Beneficiaries of my estate are as provided under the Trust.

# Article III. Pour-Over to the Trust

I give all property I own at my death, of every kind and wherever
located, to the then-acting trustee of the Trust, to be administered
and distributed under the Trust's provisions.

If the Trust is not in existence at my death, I give all such
property to my heirs at law under California's intestate succession
statutes.

# Article IV. Executor

I nominate Morgan Reyes as Executor of this Will. If Morgan Reyes is
unable or unwilling to serve, I nominate Sam Turner. My Executor
shall serve without bond and shall have all powers granted executors
under the California Probate Code, including the powers of sale and
investment.

# Article V. Guardianship

If at my death any child of mine is under eighteen, I nominate
Morgan Reyes as guardian of the person and estate of each such
child. If Morgan Reyes is unable or unwilling to serve, Morgan Reyes
may nominate a successor guardian; if no such nomination can be
made, I nominate Jordan Blake to select an appropriate guardian in
Jordan Blake's sole discretion.

# Article VI. No Contest

If any beneficiary contests this Will or the Trust, that person's
gift lapses, unless the contest was brought with probable cause as
defined by California law.
