# Spec: CIV-110 — Request for Dismissal

## Purpose

CIV-110 is how a California civil action (or part of one) ends short
of judgment: the plaintiff, cross-complainant, or — for a stipulated
dismissal — both sides ask the clerk to dismiss a complaint,
cross-complaint, or the entire action, with or without prejudice
(CCP § 581). prosaic's usual pattern is the stipulated dismissal:
the CIV-110 is prepended as a cover to a Stipulation for Dismissal
(and any [Proposed] Order) via `cover_sheet: civ110`, with the
substantive choices and both signatures completed by hand at signing.

## Promises

1. **The caption fills itself on both pages.** Standard front matter
   resolves the attorney/party block, court block, party names, and
   case number — including the page-2 caption echo, which is an
   independent set of fields. *(tested: smoke fill asserts the case
   number lands)*
2. **No dismissal decision is ever made by the machine.** The
   with-prejudice / without-prejudice / § 664.6 boxes (item 1.a), the
   what-is-dismissed boxes (item 1.b), the fee-waiver "did / did not"
   boxes, and the role checkboxes under each signature are all left
   blank for the parties. *(tested: AI visual court-readiness
   judgment — rubric explicitly requires every dismissal-type/party
   checkbox, date, and both signature blocks to render empty)*
3. **Signature blocks stay open.** Item 2 and item 3 dates and
   printed names default to blank so both sides complete them at
   signing; a typed name may be supplied deliberately, but never by
   default. *(tested: test_mandatory_blanks_survive_a_rich_fill
   (item2_date, item2_print_name, item3_date, item3_print_name) + AI
   visual court-readiness judgment (signature blocks confirmed
   empty))*
4. **Court-owned areas are untouched**: items 4–7 (clerk's use) and
   the whole page-2 fee-recovery declaration. *(untested)*
5. **The output renders identically in every viewer** — the blank's
   XFA layer is stripped after filling so no viewer shows an unfilled
   template over the filled values. *(tested indirectly:
   descriptor-blank consistency + smoke fill)*
6. **The descriptor matches the shipped blank's revision.** *(tested:
   test_descriptor_matches_blank)*

## Non-obvious constraints

- **Why so much stays blank on this form in particular:** a
  stipulated CIV-110 is signed by *both* sides, and item 3 is the
  opposing party's consent. Pre-filling any choice box, date, or name
  in another party's section puts words in their mouth on a filed
  court form. The machine's job ends at the caption.
- **The attorney block is granular** (separate name / bar number /
  firm / street / city / state / zip boxes) and only the name has an
  automatic binding; address components must be supplied per-form.
  Do not fake it by cramming a full address into the name box.
- **The SBN trap:** the name box's automatic value appends
  "(SBN …)" when a bar number is configured. Attorneys must override
  the name field and put the bar number in its own box, or the
  number prints twice — once in the name, once in the STATE BAR
  NUMBER box.
- **Field names are dotted XFA paths** verified against the blank;
  the checkboxes and radio groups are *deliberately* unmapped, not
  missing. Extending the descriptor to auto-check dismissal-type
  boxes would contradict promise 2 — a future need for that must be
  an explicit per-form, human-directed value, never a default.
- The clerk rejects a CIV-110 whose item 1.b selection conflicts
  with the caption (e.g. "entire action" when a cross-complaint
  survives); since the boxes are hand-completed, the *reviewing
  human* owns that consistency, and nothing in the fill should imply
  otherwise.
