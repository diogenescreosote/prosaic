# Spec: MC-030 — Declaration

## Purpose

MC-030 is the Judicial Council's general-purpose declaration cover
form, usable in any California case type. Any declarant — a party,
a witness, an attorney — files it to put sworn factual testimony
before the court, most often in support of or opposition to a motion.
The common pattern is to use MC-030 as a
*cover*: the box on the form says "See attached Declaration of Jane
Roe." and the full declaration, on pleading paper, rides behind it.
prosaic exists to make that pattern one line of front matter
(`cover_sheet: mc030`) on the declaration source.

## Promises

1. **The caption fills itself.** Given the standard caption front
   matter (filer, court, petitioner, respondent, case number), every
   caption field — attorney/party block, court block, party names,
   case number — resolves without per-form input.
   *(tested: smoke fill asserts the case number lands in a field)*
2. **The body defaults to the cover-sheet idiom.** With no explicit
   body, the form reads "See attached Declaration of \<name\>.", and
   the declarant's printed name is parsed from the paper title
   ("DECLARATION OF JANE ROE …" → "JANE ROE"). *(tested:
   test_declarant_from_title)*
3. **A long body becomes a proper attachment.** Body text that cannot
   fit the box produces "See Attachment 1." plus an appended MC-025
   captioned for the case — never shrunk to illegibility, never
   truncated. *(tested: test_overflow_spills_to_mc025)*
4. **Nothing is signed, dated, or role-declared by the machine.** The
   date line, the signature, and the declarant-role checkboxes
   (Attorney / Plaintiff / Petitioner / Defendant / Respondent /
   Other) are left for the human at signing time. *(tested:
   test_mandatory_blanks_survive_a_rich_fill (date; the registry-wide
   sweep now covers MC-030) + AI visual court-readiness judgment
   (date/signature/role checkboxes confirmed empty) + the calibration
   negative control test_ai_judge_calibration_fails_sabotaged_output,
   which sabotages MC-030's date field specifically and confirms the
   judge catches it — partial: the role checkboxes have no mapped
   widget, so only the visual judgment covers them)*
5. **The filing copy carries no interactive chrome** — privacy
   banners and Save/Print/Clear buttons are stripped. *(tested: AI
   visual court-readiness judgment — the shared rubric requires no
   leftover interactive buttons or privacy banners on every rendered
   form, MC-030 included; this is a soft scoring criterion, not one
   of the judge's hard failures)*
6. **The descriptor's revision is verified**, and the suite fails if
   the shipped blank drifts from the map. *(tested:
   test_descriptor_matches_blank)*

## Non-obvious constraints

- **The AcroForm field names carry no meaning.** MC-030's fields are
  named `FillText35`, `FillText22`, etc.; the numbers do not track
  position or purpose. Every mapping was established by filling each
  field with its own name and rendering. Never extend or "correct"
  the map by name inference.
- **The role checkboxes are deliberately unmapped**, not forgotten.
  Which capacity the declarant signs in is a legal statement that
  belongs to the person signing, made by hand at the same moment as
  the signature and date. Automating it invites a declaration signed
  in the wrong capacity.
- **The date field stays empty even when the signing date is
  "known."** A pre-printed date that differs from the actual signing
  date makes the perjury declaration inaccurate on its face. The
  print-name line, by contrast, *is* pre-filled — typing a name
  creates no false statement, and it leaves the human only the
  signature and date to complete.
- **`other_role_specify` stays blank** unless the declarant genuinely
  signs in a capacity the printed checkboxes don't cover; filling it
  while a standard role applies muddles the declaration.
- The overflow attachment is labeled **Attachment 1** because the
  body is the form's only substantive item; the label must match what
  the box's "See Attachment N." text says.
