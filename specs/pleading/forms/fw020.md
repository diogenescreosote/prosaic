# Spec: FW-020 — Request for Court Reporter by Party With Fee Waiver

## Purpose

FW-020 asks the court to provide an official court reporter, at no fee,
for a hearing or trial that the court would not otherwise report (Cal.
Rules of Court, rule 2.956(c)(2); Gov. Code § 68086), by a party who
has a fee waiver or files an FW-001 with it. Approved for **optional**
use. Many courts supply no reporter in civil, family or probate
matters; without this request those proceedings go unreported and no
transcript can be made. Registered as `fw020`; built on `technology:
overlay` (ADR-0046). One page.

## Promises

1. **One proceeding per form.** Item 2 has one date box and a trial /
   hearing pair; the descriptor fills one date, and the agent guide
   tells the filer to file one form per non-consecutive day. *(tested:
   test_fw020_fills_one_proceeding_and_leaves_signing_to_the_filer)*
2. **Signing is the filer's.** The date and signature lines are e-sign
   areas for `filer`, never machine-filled. *(tested: same)*
3. **The output is flattened**, the Print/Save/Clear buttons and the
   privacy banner are stripped, and the banner's static gray underlay
   is painted out. *(tested: the FORMS-parametrized suites)*
4. **The descriptor matches the shipped blank's revision** (FW-020 [New
   January 1, 2021]). *(tested: test_descriptor_matches_blank)*

## Non-obvious constraints

- The two item-1 checkboxes are both named `Checkbox1[0]` under
  different parents (`LI1`, `LI2`), with on-states `/1` and `/2`; the
  full widget paths keep them distinct.
- The signature line has no widget; its e-sign rect is hand-authored
  from the rule at x 354--576, 97.9 pt from the bottom. Re-verify with
  `sc form preview fw020` after any blank-revision bump.
- The item-2 date box is about 107 pt wide; two dates do not fit.
