# Spec: EFS-020 — Proposed Order (Cover Sheet)

## Purpose

When a proposed order is electronically filed in a California court
that requires it, Cal. Rules of Court 2.252 and 3.1312 demand a cover
sheet identifying the proposed order and the proceeding it resolves —
EFS-020 is that cover sheet. Any party lodging a proposed order files
it, in any case type. prosaic's pattern: `cover_sheet: efs020` on
a [Proposed] Order source prepends the filled form to the rendered
order.

## Promises

1. **The caption and the two identifying items fill themselves.**
   Standard front matter resolves the caption; item 1 (party
   submitting the order) defaults to the filer, the natural value for
   a self-represented party; item 2 (title of the proposed order)
   defaults to the paper title. *(tested: smoke fill asserts the case
   number lands)*
2. **The proceeding block (item 3) is honest about hearings.** Its
   description / date-time / place fields are supplied per-form when
   there was a hearing, and may stay blank (or "N/A") for orders that
   resolve the action without one — e.g. an order on a stipulated
   dismissal. The form never invents hearing details. *(untested)*
3. **The signature area stays open**: the page-1 date, signature, and
   type-or-print name line are left for hand completion at filing.
   *(tested: test_mandatory_blanks_survive_a_rich_fill (print_name) +
   AI visual court-readiness judgment (date/signature confirmed
   empty))*
4. **Page 2 — the Proof of Electronic Service — is entirely
   untouched**, including its single checkbox: service is attested by
   the person or e-filing service provider who actually serves, after
   filing, not by the drafting machine. *(tested: AI visual
   court-readiness judgment — rubric explicitly requires page 2 to
   render entirely blank)*
5. **Every viewer shows the filled values** — the XFA layer is
   stripped after filling. *(tested indirectly: descriptor-blank
   consistency + smoke fill)*
6. **The descriptor matches the shipped blank's revision.** *(tested:
   test_descriptor_matches_blank)*

## Non-obvious constraints

- **The form is a cover, not the order.** Rule 3.1312 also requires
  an *editable word-processing copy* of the proposed order to reach
  the court separately. Producing the cover sheet without the
  editable copy (prosaic's envelope system can emit a `.docx`
  alongside the PDF) leaves the filing incomplete; the spec of this
  form includes remembering that companion obligation.
- **Item 1 names a party, not a lawyer.** The automatic default
  (filer name) is only right for self-represented filers; a
  represented party's fill must override item 1 with the party's own
  name. Leaving counsel's name there misidentifies who submits the
  order.
- **The SBN trap** (shared with the other granular-caption forms):
  the name box's auto value appends "(SBN …)" when a bar number is
  set; attorneys override the name and use the dedicated bar-number
  box, or the number prints twice.
- **The attorney block is granular** — address components have no
  automatic parsing and are supplied per-form.
- **The page-2 caption echo ("CASE NAME") has no automatic
  binding** on this form; it must be supplied explicitly, or page 2
  goes out with an empty caption over the (blank) proof of service.
- **Judicial-officer and department caption fields** belong to the
  court's calendar, not the filer's aspirations: fill them only from
  actual assignment information, never from hoped-for values.
