# Spec: MC-040 — Notice of Change of Address or Other Contact Information

## Purpose

MC-040 notifies the court and the other parties that a party's or
attorney's address or other contact information has changed (Cal.
Rules of Court, rules 2.200, 8.816). Page 1 is the notice; page 2 is
a built-in Proof of Service by First-Class Mail. It is the pilot
form for `technology: overlay` (ADR-0033): no AcroForm values are
ever set — every value is drawn as page content at its widget's
rectangle and the output is flattened.

## Promises

1. **The output is flattened.** No AcroForm dictionary, no widget
   annotations, no interactive chrome — identical rendering in every
   viewer. *(tested: test_overlay_forms.test_overlay_output_is_flattened)*
2. **Every drawn value lands inside its widget's rectangle**, and
   checkbox marks land inside their boxes. *(tested:
   test_values_land_inside_their_widget_rects,
   test_checkbox_mark_lands_inside_its_box)*
3. **The item-2 new-contact block keeps one type size.** Its fields
   share `size_group: new_contact`: when any line shrinks to fit, the
   block shrinks together. *(tested:
   test_size_group_members_share_a_size)*
4. **E-sign areas are declared and typed.** `esign_parties: [filer,
   server]`; the page-1 signature/date belong to the filer, the POS
   declarant fields and signature to the server, with types drawn from
   the ADR-0033 taxonomy. The geometry preview renders them
   party-colored and type-labeled, warning-free. *(tested:
   test_geometry_preview_draws_clean,
   test_esign_taxonomy_and_parties_are_validated)*
5. **The header block carries the NEW contact information** (the form
   is the notice of it) via `atty_party_block`; the old address
   appears nowhere.
6. **The POS declarant fields stay blank for a non-party server** —
   CCP 1013a bars a party from serving their own notice, so
   `pos_declarant_address`, mail date/place, the service-method
   checkboxes, and the POS signature block are reserved (typed e-sign
   areas for the `server` party); only the served-person blocks are
   machine-fillable. *(untested; defaults are `""` and the agent guide
   carries the rule)*
7. **The descriptor matches the shipped blank's revision**
   (MC-040 [Rev. January 1, 2013]). *(tested:
   test_descriptor_matches_blank)*

## Non-obvious constraints

- **Widget pairs share names with different on-states**:
  `Representative[0]` ("self-represented party") turns on with `/1`
  while `Representative[1]` ("the attorney for") uses `/2`; the two
  POS `Service[0]` boxes likewise `/1` vs `/2`. Irrelevant to the
  overlay fill (marks are drawn, not set) but load-bearing for anyone
  reverting this form to acroform technology.
- **The signature lines have no widgets.** Their e-sign rects
  (`signature`, `pos_signature`) are hand-authored and verified with
  `sc form preview mc040` — re-verify there after any blank-revision
  bump.
