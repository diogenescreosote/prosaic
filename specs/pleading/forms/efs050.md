# Spec: POS-050/EFS-050 — Proof of Electronic Service

## Purpose

POS-050/EFS-050 proves electronic service of documents (Cal. Rules of
Court, rule 2.251; CCP § 1010.6). One page: declarant's addresses,
exact titles of the documents served, one served-person block with
electronic address and date, perjury declaration. Registered as
`efs050`; built on `technology: overlay` (ADR-0033). The process
around it — verification against the sender's mailbox before
papering, canonical source naming — is the
`prove-electronic-service` skill.

## Promises

1. **A party can be the declarant.** The form's declaration is "I am
   at least 18 years old," with no not-a-party recital — the
   descriptor and agent guide preserve that distinction from the
   mail-service forms, whose proofs a party may not sign. *(the
   blank's own face; re-verify on any revision bump)*
2. **The output is flattened** (no AcroForm, no widgets, chrome gone)
   and every value draws inside its widget's rectangle, per the
   overlay-technology invariants tested registry-wide.
   *(tested: the FORMS-parametrized suites in test_forms.py /
   test_forms_adversarial.py cover efs050 automatically)*
3. **The item-3 served block keeps one type size** (`size_group:
   served`) and accepts several e-addresses for one office in the
   address field; a second *person* requires the POS-050(P)
   attachment checkbox, which is mapped.
4. **The caption fills from standard front matter**, including the
   granular attorney block (filer_name_bare/filer_firm/… autos), the
   DEPARTMENT and JUDICIAL OFFICER boxes (hearing_dept / judge).
5. **The signature area is a declared e-sign field** (party
   `declarant`, types date/signature), hand-rect for the widgetless
   signature line, verified via `sc form preview efs050`. sig_date
   and the signature always stay blank for the declarant.
6. **The descriptor matches the shipped blank's revision**
   (POS-050/EFS-050 [Rev. February 1, 2017]). *(tested:
   test_descriptor_matches_blank)*

## Non-obvious constraints

- **One send is one proof, and a superseded send gets none.** The form
  has a single `On (date)` and no field for a time, so a proof attests
  to one set of documents served on named people on one date. Two
  emails are two proofs even when they go out the same day, minutes
  apart, to the same people — a merged proof would attest, under
  penalty of perjury, to a service that never happened as described.
  When a corrected version of a document follows the original, only
  the corrected send is proved; the erroneous copy appears in no proof
  at all. A morning that went

  ```
  09:00  email 1 --> FORM-A, FORM-B, FORM-C, FORM-D
  09:30  email 2 --> FORM-A (corrected), FORM-B (corrected)
  ```

  yields **two** proofs, both dated that day: email 1's lists FORM-C
  and FORM-D only (A and B omitted — superseded thirty minutes later);
  email 2's lists the corrected FORM-A and FORM-B. Identical dates on
  the two proofs are correct, not a defect. Courtesy attachments (a
  blank form sent for the other side's convenience) are not served
  documents and do not belong in `efs050_documents`.

- The blank is an XFA-hybrid whose fields live under
  `topmostSubform[0]`; one checkbox hides in an `#area[0]` subform.
  Names verified against the /TU dump; the usual no-name-inference
  rule applies.
- Item 2 wants **exact document titles** — the proof is impeachable
  by comparison to the served email, so the title string should be
  copied from the served document's face, not paraphrased.
