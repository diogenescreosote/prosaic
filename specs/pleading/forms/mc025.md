# Spec: MC-025 — Attachment to Judicial Council Form

## Purpose

MC-025 is the universal continuation sheet: the form's own face says
it "may be used with any Judicial Council form." Whenever an answer
is longer than the box a JC form provides, California practice is to
write "See Attachment N." in the box and put the full text on an
MC-025 identifying the case and the parent item number. Any filer of
any JC form may need one; prosaic uses it two ways — as the
automatic target of every overflow anywhere in the form engine, and
as a directly fillable form for deliberate attachments.

## Promises

1. **Overflow anywhere lands here correctly.** When any field on any
   registered form overflows, the appended MC-025 carries the parent
   item's number in "ATTACHMENT (Number):", the full untruncated
   text in the body, and a short caption (SHORT TITLE + CASE NUMBER)
   resolved from the same case metadata as the parent form. *(tested:
   test_overflow_spills_to_mc025; the cover-form overflow scenario asserts
   the appended page carries the case number; AI judgment on
   attachment usability)*
2. **The short title composes itself** as "\<petitioner\> v.
   \<respondent\>" from the standard caption front matter, and can be
   overridden for cases with a different short caption (e.g.
   "Estate of Roe"). *(tested via overflow scenarios)*
3. **The body never becomes illegible.** Text shrinks to fit the
   page-sized body area but never below 8 pt; text that still cannot
   fit is a reported failure, not a silent truncation. *(tested:
   test_multipage_overflow_preserves_every_sentence confirms overflow
   text is never silently truncated, even when it spans multiple
   attachment pages; AI visual court-readiness judgment confirms body
   legibility on a direct fill — partial: the below-8pt floor itself
   still has no direct font-size assertion)*
4. **Direct fills work like any form**: supply `attachment_number`
   and `body`, and the caption resolves automatically. *(tested:
   smoke fill)*
5. **The descriptor matches the shipped blank's revision**, verified
   loudly. *(tested: test_descriptor_matches_blank)*

## Non-obvious constraints

- **The logical names `attachment_number` and `body` are engine
  contract.** The overflow path fills exactly those two data fields
  on this descriptor; renaming them breaks every form's overflow
  behavior at once. Treat them as frozen API.
- **There is no signature or date line on this form — and that is
  correct.** The perjury recital at the foot is static form text: if
  the parent item is declared under penalty of perjury, the
  attachment is covered by the parent's signature. Do not "fix" the
  form by adding signature handling.
- **One page is all the engine gives you.** The body area covers
  nearly the whole single page; the engine does not paginate a
  too-long attachment onto a second MC-025 automatically. A filing
  needing multiple attachment pages is a human/agent decision —
  which is also when the "Page \_\_ of \_\_" fields (normally left
  blank for single-attachment filings) earn values.
- **The attachment number is the parent's item number, not a serial
  counter.** "10c" is valid; the value must match whatever the
  parent form's box says after "See Attachment", or the clerk (and
  later readers) cannot connect the two.
- **Field names (`FillText4` etc.) carry no meaning**; every mapping
  was verified empirically. Extend only by fill-render-inspect.
