# Spec: MC-050 — Substitution of Attorney—Civil (Without Court Order)

## Purpose

MC-050 changes who represents a party in a pending case — attorney to
attorney, attorney to self-represented, or self-represented to
attorney — without asking the court for an order (Code Civ. Proc.
§§ 284(1), 285; Cal. Rules of Court, rule 3.1362). It only works when
every person whose consent it needs is willing to sign: the party
being represented, the outgoing (former) attorney, and the incoming
(new) attorney or self-represented party. If the outgoing attorney
will not consent, MC-050 cannot be used at all; the remedy is a
noticed motion to be relieved as counsel (CCP § 284(2)), a different
filing this descriptor does not cover.

## Promises

1. **The caption fills itself, on both pages.** Standard front matter
   resolves the attorney/party block, court block, and the CASE NAME /
   CASE NUMBER boxes. Those two boxes are each a single AcroForm field
   with a widget on page 1 and a second widget on the page-2 caption
   echo, so one descriptor entry fills both. *(tested: smoke fill
   asserts the case number lands; a rich fill was rendered and visually
   confirmed on both pages during authoring)*
2. **No substitution decision is made by the machine.** Which side was
   self-represented before and after (former_rep_party_self /
   former_rep_attorney / new_rep_party_self / new_rep_attorney), the
   substituting party's role (plaintiff / defendant / petitioner /
   respondent / other), and the former/new attorney's identifying
   details all require an explicit, human-directed value — none has a
   default other than blank. *(tested: test_mandatory_blanks_survive_a_rich_fill
   asserts an unset other_role_specify or role checkbox stays inert)*
3. **Consent is never fabricated.** Items 4-6 are three independent
   consent paragraphs — the party, the former attorney, and the new
   attorney — each with its own date and printed-name field (no
   signature widget exists on any JC form in this registry; those are
   wet-ink only). All six fields (item4_date, item4_print_name,
   item5_date, item5_print_name, item6_date, item6_print_name) and both
   of items 5/6's own consent-applies checkboxes default empty/unchecked
   and stay that way through a rich fill. *(tested:
   test_mc050_consent_and_service_fields_stay_blank)*
4. **The Proof of Service by Mail (page 2) records nothing that hasn't
   happened yet.** The mailing date, the server's declaration date, and
   the server's own printed name all default blank — filling them ahead
   of the actual mailing would misstate an event under penalty of
   perjury. *(tested: test_mc050_consent_and_service_fields_stay_blank)*
5. **The filing copy carries no interactive chrome** — the privacy
   banner and Save/Print/Clear buttons are stripped from both pages.
   *(tested indirectly: descriptor-blank consistency + smoke fill;
   visually confirmed during authoring)*
6. **The descriptor's revision is verified**, and the suite fails if the
   shipped blank drifts from the map. *(tested: test_descriptor_matches_blank)*

## Non-obvious constraints

- **The checkbox names on this form are inconsistent with each other,
  not just meaningless.** The five item-3 role checkboxes are named
  `CheckBox7`, `CheckBx7`, `ChckBx7`, `CheckB7`, and `Check7` — no two
  spelled the same way — and item 1/2's pairs are `CheckBox11`/
  `CheckBx11` and `CheckBox9`/`CheckBx9`. Every mapping here came from
  checking one box at a time and rasterizing; none was inferred from
  the name.
- **CASE NAME and CASE NUMBER are shared fields, not per-page
  duplicates.** Unlike CIV-110 (an XFA form with distinct dotted field
  names per page), MC-050 is a flat AcroForm where the same field
  object has two widget "Kids" — this descriptor has no `_p2` entries
  for the caption, and adding them would be redundant, not more
  correct.
- **Item 4 has no checkbox; items 5 and 6 do.** Item 4 (the party's own
  consent) is asked of everyone unconditionally. Items 5 and 6 are
  each preceded by a checkbox because the paragraph itself may not
  apply: item 5 (former attorney's consent) is moot when item 1 was
  "Party represented self" (no former attorney exists to consent), and
  item 6 (new attorney's consent) is moot when item 2 was "Party is
  representing self." The checkbox is the former/new attorney's own
  affirmative act, made when they sign — never set by this descriptor.
- **"Party is representing self*" carries a real legal restriction.**
  The form's own footnote lists categories — guardians, conservators,
  trustees, personal representatives, probate fiduciaries,
  corporations, unincorporated associations — that generally cannot
  represent themselves. The agent_guide flags this as a caveat for a
  human to catch; it is not, and cannot be, enforced in code, because
  the descriptor has no way to know the substituting party's legal
  capacity.
- **The Proof of Service recipient slots (pos_recipient_1..5) are the
  one part of page 2 that MAY be filled ahead of time**, once the
  actual service list is known — unlike the date/declarant fields,
  naming who will be served is not itself a statement that the mailing
  already happened. There is no MC-025 overflow wiring for a service
  list longer than the five slots the form provides; pos_continued_in_attachment
  exists for a human to check by hand alongside a hand-prepared
  continuation page.
