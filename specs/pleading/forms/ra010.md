# Spec: RA-010 — Notice of Remote Appearance

## Purpose

RA-010 tells the court, and gives the other parties notice, that a
party, an attorney, or a witness intends to appear remotely in a civil
case. Its use is **mandatory** (CCP § 367.75; Cal. Rules of Court,
rule 3.672(f)–(h)) unless the court's website describes an online
procedure for giving notice instead. Registered as `ra010`; built on
`technology: overlay` (ADR-0046).

Three pages. Page 1: caption, then who will appear remotely (item 1),
throughout the case or at one described proceeding (item 2), by
videoconference or audio (item 3), and — for an evidentiary hearing or
trial only — what further aspects of the proceeding the party asks to
have conducted remotely, and why (item 4). Page 2: a short caption, the
confidentiality undertaking (item 5) and its signature, then a
Declaration of Notice grid recording how and when each other party or
person entitled to notice was told, with its own perjury signature.
Page 3: instructions, which the form itself marks "need not be filed".

### The rule, summarized

- **Notice is the motion.** For an evidentiary hearing or trial, the
  notice serves as the party's motion under CCP § 367.75(d) (rule
  3.672(h)(2)(B)). No separate noticed motion is required to ask for
  remote testimony.
- **Evidentiary hearing or trial on 15 or more court days' notice**
  (and every small claims trial): file RA-010 and give notice to the
  other parties at least **10 court days** before the hearing or trial
  (rule 3.672(h)(2)(C)). On shorter notice, rule 3.672(h)(2)(D)
  applies: the short-notice procedure of (g)(2)(B), or filing RA-010
  with notice received at least 5 court days before.
- **Proceedings without testimony** follow rule 3.672(g): at least 2
  court days before a proceeding noticed 3 or more court days out;
  on shorter notice, with the moving papers (the moving party) or by
  2 p.m. the court day before (anyone else).
- **Opposition** to remote proceedings at an evidentiary hearing or
  trial is on RA-015, served and filed at least 5 court days before
  (15 or more days' notice) or by noon the court day before (shorter
  notice) (rule 3.672(h)(3)).
- **The court decides**, and may require anyone to appear in person
  (CCP § 367.75(b)); RA-020 is the optional-use order form.

The form's page 3 states the 2022 deadlines; rule 3.672 was amended
effective August 4, 2023, so the agent guide summarizes the rule, not
page 3.

## Promises

1. **A fill emits the filed pages only.** The descriptor declares
   `filed_pages: "1-2"`; page 3 (instructions) is dropped from every
   fill, standalone or as a `cover_sheet:`, and an explicit
   `--pages 1-3` keeps it for reference. The e-sign sidecar describes
   the pages actually written. *(tested:
   test_ra010_files_two_pages_and_keeps_the_instructions_on_request)*
2. **Item 1's "Other (name and role in case)" is the place for a
   nonparty witness who will testify remotely.** The descriptor maps
   both the line beside the box and the multiline continuation under
   it, and the agent guide says so.
3. **Every Declaration-of-Notice block lands in its own boxes** despite
   the blank recycling leaf names across blocks (rows 3/5/7/8 share
   `T162`–`T165`; rows 4/6 share `CheckBox24`/`Field2`/`T166`–`T169`;
   row 5's parent is literally `List5\.1`). *(tested:
   test_ra010_grid_blocks_land_in_their_own_boxes_and_signing_stays_blank)*
4. **Item 2's a/b boxes are distinct** though both are leaf `Ch1`; they
   differ by parent and on-state (`/1`, `/2`). *(tested: same)*
5. **Nothing is signed or dated by the machine.** Both signature dates
   and both signatures stay blank and are declared e-sign fields (party
   `filer`), so DocuSeal geometry flows into the build's
   `.fields.json`. The widgetless signature rects rest on the blank's
   own rules. The print-name lines pre-fill from the filer. *(tested:
   test_esign_geometry.test_form_fill_writes_a_build_sidecar_on_the_rules)*
6. **Item 4 never truncates.** Text that does not fit spills to an
   MC-025 "Attachment 4", which is what the form's own note ("attach
   form MC-25 if more space is needed") asks for.
7. **The output is flattened**, and the caption fills from standard
   front matter on both pages. *(tested: the FORMS-parametrized suites
   in test_forms.py / test_forms_adversarial.py)*
8. **The descriptor matches the shipped blank's revision** (RA-010 [New
   January 1, 2022]). *(tested: test_descriptor_matches_blank)*

## Non-obvious constraints

- **The face numbers the last two grid blocks "7." and "7."** The
  bottom-right block is the eighth; its keys are `notice8_*`.
- The "more people were given notice" box asks for a separate MC-025
  titled "Attachment Notice"; checking it does not create one.
- The confidentiality box (item 5) is the signer's own undertaking and
  is set only on the signer's instruction.
- The page-2 caption has no OTHER CASE NAME line; page 1's is mapped
  but has no front-matter binding.
- Item 2b's proceeding fields are not bound to the front matter's
  hearing keys: with item 2a (throughout the case) they must stay
  empty, and a source's hearing may not be the proceeding the notice
  is about.
