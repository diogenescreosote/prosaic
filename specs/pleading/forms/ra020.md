# Spec: RA-020 — Order Regarding Remote Appearance

## Purpose

RA-020 is the court's order on remote appearance at a proceeding in a
civil case (CCP § 367.75; Cal. Rules of Court, rule 3.672), approved
for **optional** use. It answers a Notice of Remote Appearance (RA-010)
and any Opposition (RA-015): which proceeding (item 1), who must appear
or testify in person (item 2), who may appear or testify remotely
(item 3a) and through which technology, failing which they appear in
person (item 3b), and any other orders (item 4). A party may lodge it
filled as a proposed order; the court may use it, its own minute order,
or neither. Registered as `ra020`; built on `technology: overlay`
(ADR-0046). One page.

## Promises

1. **The judicial signature block is never filled.** The Date line and
   the JUDGE OR JUDICIAL OFFICER line are mapped (so tests can prove
   them empty) and always stay blank; the descriptor declares no e-sign
   party, so no sidecar is written and nothing invites an e-signature
   on the court's line. *(tested:
   test_ra020_judicial_signature_block_is_never_filled)*
2. **Names stay level with their roles, under their headings.** Items
   2 and 3a are each one pair of multiline boxes (Name, Role in Case);
   the pair shares a `size_group`, top anchoring, and one person per
   line, and every line is centered under its column's printed heading
   (each heading sits at its box's horizontal center on the blank).
   *(tested: test_ra020_columns_center_under_their_headings_and_stay_level)*
3. **Item 4 never truncates**: overflow spills to an MC-025
   "Attachment 4".
4. **The output is flattened**, the Print/Save/Clear buttons and the
   privacy banner are stripped, and the banner's static gray underlay
   is painted out. *(tested: the FORMS-parametrized suites)*
5. **The descriptor matches the shipped blank's revision** (RA-020 [New
   January 1, 2022]). *(tested: test_descriptor_matches_blank)*

## Non-obvious constraints

- Whether anyone appears in person or remotely is the court's decision;
  a proposed order states what the party asks for, and the agent guide
  says so.
- A name long enough to wrap pushes every later line of its column
  down a line, out of step with the role column. Keep entries short;
  put detail in item 4.
- The caption carries the lodging party's attorney/party block, as on
  any proposed order on a Judicial Council form.
